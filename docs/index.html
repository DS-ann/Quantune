More than playback

Your music, connected.

Tools for the way you listen: alone, with friends, with lyrics on screen, or with your own library close at hand.

Listen Together

Create online or same-Wi-Fi rooms, approve guests, share playback and chat while listening. The host leads track changes; guests can control play and pause.

Lyrics that follow

Follow line-synchronized lyrics or word-timed karaoke, adjust timing and use translation controls from the listening experience.

Recognize a song

Identify nearby music with audio-fingerprint matching, then try opening the match in Quantune playback when a playable result is available.

Your personal library

Keep local tracks, playlists, favourites and listening history alongside supported online music and cached tracks.

Make the player yours

Customize themes, typography, artwork, playback controls, gestures, visualizer styles and lyrics presentation.

Music at a glance

Use resizable home-screen widgets to see track details and reach playback controls without opening the app.

Stay with the song

Hear it. Read it. Feel the timing.

Quantune supports plain lyrics, line-synchronized lyrics and word-timed karaoke formats. You can adjust synchronization and configure translation options from the app.

  • Timed lyrics support including LRC, TTML, QRC and YRC.
  • Multiple lyrics-provider integrations, subject to availability.
  • Optional translation through a provider and model you configure.

Good to know

Clear answers before you install.

Which Android versions are supported?

Quantune's minimum supported version is Android 6.0 (API 23). The published APKs are built for ARMv7a and ARM64 devices. x86 and x86_64 packages are not provided.

Does Listen Together stream the host's audio?

No. Listen Together synchronizes track information and playback state; it does not relay the host's audio. Each participant resolves and plays the shared track on their own device.

Does Song Recognizer upload my microphone recording?

The feature creates a compact audio fingerprint on the device and sends that fingerprint for matching. The raw microphone recording is not uploaded by this feature. Microphone permission is needed when recognition is used.

Do lyrics translation and online music need other services?

Yes. Online music, lyrics and recognition depend on third-party services and their availability. AI lyrics translation uses the provider you configure; some providers require their own API key or paid account.

Can I use my local music?

Yes. Quantune can browse and play supported local audio files and organize playlists, favourites and listening history. Offline caching depends on the track, provider and successful cache or download operation.

Where can I report a problem?

Open a GitHub issue and include your device model, Android version, Quantune release, APK architecture and steps to reproduce the problem. Remove private information from logs before sharing.
