Before you install

Good to know.

Which Android versions are supported?

Quantune's minimum supported version is Android 6.0 (API 23). Published APKs support ARMv7a and ARM64 devices.

Does Listen Together stream the host's audio?

No. It synchronizes track information and playback state. Each participant resolves and plays the track on their own device.

Does Song Recognition upload my recording?

The recognition flow creates a compact audio fingerprint on the device and sends that fingerprint for matching. The raw microphone recording is not uploaded by this feature.

Can I play local music?

Yes. Quantune can browse and play supported local audio files and organize playlists, favourites and listening history.

Do online music and lyrics depend on other services?

Yes. Online music, lyrics, recognition and translation depend on third-party services and their availability. Some translation providers require their own API key or paid account.

Where can I report a problem?

Open a GitHub issue with your device model, Android version, Quantune release, APK architecture and steps to reproduce. Remove private information from logs before sharing.
